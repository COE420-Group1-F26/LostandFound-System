# Project Description

## Problem Statement

Lost items on university campuses are currently handled through informal
channels such as social media groups, notice boards, and walk-in visits to
security offices. Found items are stored without a searchable record, so
owners often have no way of knowing an item has been recovered. Items are
frequently discarded or remain unclaimed even when the owner is actively
looking for them.

## Intended Users

- Students and staff who have lost an item on campus
- Students and staff who have found an item and wish to hand it in
- Campus security and administrative staff who receive, store, and release items

## Main Features

- User registration and login with university credentials
- Lost item reports, including description, category, location, and date
- Found item entries submitted by finders or recorded by security staff
- Automatic matching that suggests likely pairs between lost and found reports
- Search and filtering by category, location, and date range
- Notifications sent to a user when a possible match is identified
- A claim process in which the owner verifies ownership before release
- An administrative dashboard for staff to manage item records and claim status

## Key Functionalities

- Create, view, update, and close lost and found reports
- Compare submitted reports and rank possible matches by similarity
- Track each item through its lifecycle: reported, matched, claimed, returned
- Maintain a record of every claim, including who released the item and when
- Generate summary reports on item volume, claim rates, and unclaimed items
