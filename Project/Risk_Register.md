# Risk Register

The following risks were identified for the Lost and Found Property Claim
System. Probability and impact are assessed on a Low / Medium / High scale.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Possible Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation / Response Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Project requirements change after development has started | Team or stakeholder feedback reveals missing features once early increments are demonstrated | Medium | Medium | The incremental process model was selected partly for this reason: changes are absorbed into a later increment rather than forcing rework of completed features. Scope.md is treated as a living document and is updated whenever a change is agreed. |
| R2 | An item is released to someone who is not the rightful owner | Ownership verification relies only on a claim form with no secondary check | Medium | High | The claim process requires the claimant's AUS ID together with identifying details that are not shown publicly in the listing, such as unique markings or contents. Campus security approves every release through the admin dashboard, so no item is released automatically by the system. |
| R3 | Personal data submitted by users is exposed | The system stores AUS IDs, contact details, and uploaded photographs without adequate access control | Low | High | Access to the admin dashboard is restricted to authenticated campus security accounts. Only the minimum personal data needed to process a claim is collected, and identifying details supplied during verification are never displayed in public listings. |
| R4 | The matching feature suggests poor or irrelevant matches | Matching is text-based only and depends on how users describe items, which varies widely | High | Medium | Matching is deliberately positioned as a suggestion rather than a decision, and image recognition is explicitly out of scope for this version. Manual search and browse remain fully available, so a weak match never prevents a user from finding their item. |
| R5 | A team member becomes unavailable close to a deadline | Examinations, illness, or personal commitments during the semester | Medium | Medium | Each member owns distinct files in the repository, so work is visible and can be picked up by another member. Internal deadlines are set ahead of the official due date to leave buffer time. |
| R6 | Development takes longer than estimated and features are left incomplete | The team is unfamiliar with parts of the chosen technology stack, particularly the database and notification components | Medium | High | Features are prioritised so that reporting, search, and claim verification are completed first; notifications are built last and can be reduced to a simpler form if time runs short. The incremental model ensures a working subset exists at every stage. |
